GW2 Wayfinder

Manage keys

    Wayfinder answers questions about Guild Wars 2 and your account. It needs a read-only GW2 API key.

    Create one at account.arena.net/applications. Select the permissions you want Wayfinder to use, for example account, characters, inventories, wallet and progression.

    Cancel

    Your key stays in your browser and is sent only to this server, which uses it to read your account from the GW2 API. Account, character and guild names are replaced with aliases before they reach the AI model. See the privacy notice.

    GW2 Wayfinder is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by ArenaNet or NCSOFT. Answers are generated by AI and can be wrong.
